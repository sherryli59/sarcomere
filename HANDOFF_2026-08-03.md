## Executive summary

The 2026-08-02 handoff blamed the failed 1,280-actin pilot on nonspecific titin
capture and prescribed an ACTN2-support gate. That gate was implemented and
works exactly as specified — **zero unsupported associations in every run
since** — but it was not the blocking defect.

Three real defects were found and fixed, and one long-standing conclusion was
overturned:

1. **Motor drive had drifted 8x too high.** 141 heads/half at full tonic
   activation vs the minimal cell's 35 at floor 0 / fraction 0.5. Myosin
   occupied the entire thin filament, the crosslinkable segment left for ACTN2
   collapsed to a median of zero by 15 ms, and the Z-disc network starved.
2. **The AM angular tether was silently weakened** by the same head cut, because
   it is scaled by `min(1, bound_heads / am_tether_saturation_heads)`.
   Compensating with `--am_alignment_k=120` restores it.
3. **The myomesin M-band spring was dead code** — forces computed and discarded.
   `--k_mm` had never done anything in any trajectory. Now fixed and tested.

**Current best configuration** (reproducible at 1,280 actins, both seeds):
reduced drive + `am_alignment_k=120` + the titin gate gives nematic order
S = 0.84–0.89, A-A register 0.10 um on target, a surviving and maturing ACTN2
network, zero unsupported associations, zero ruptures, force caps ~1e-5.

**Still unsolved:** no Z-disc banding in any configuration.

## Key measurements

### Motor drive is the assembly bottleneck

Head occupancy, not sliding speed, is the controlling variable. ACTN2 fraction
is perfectly monotonic in heads-per-actin and non-monotonic in drift:

| config | drift um/s | links pk→end | xlink ratio end | ACTN2 frac | heads/actin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline 141, floor 1, frac 1 | 3.13 | 71 → 55 | 0.14 | 0.097 | 14.3 |
| activation only (141, 0, 0.5) | 0.25 | 97 → 77 | 0.25 | 0.128 | 6.9 |
| heads only (35, 1, 1) | 1.96 | 107 → 107 | 0.34 | 0.186 | 3.1 |
| both (35, 0, 0.5) | 0.49 | 127 → 122 | 0.54 | 0.223 | 1.4 |

### Alignment torque

`am_alignment_k` is an accelerant, not a requirement — k=30 does reach S = 0.77
by 200 ms, but only in 1 of 2 seeds (0.769 vs 0.324). k=120 reaches S = 0.84
by 75 ms deterministically. The tether is reciprocal (torques myosin too) and
polarity-blind, so antiparallel geometry survives.

| | S actin | status-2 frac | A-A overlap | support |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| k=30 | 0.16–0.77 (seed-dependent) | 0.145 | 0.1050 ± 0.0100 | 0.784 |
| **k=120** | **0.843** | 0.117 | **0.1010** | 0.870 |
| k=480 | 0.929 | 0.102 | 0.1035 ± 0.0101 | 0.970 |

k=120 is the defensible magnitude: cutting heads 141 → 35 reduced
`angular_occupancy` ~4x, so ~4x compensation restores what the coarse-graining
removed. k=480 is 16x with no such justification.

### Myomesin M-band — first run ever

Fourier amplitude of positions along x, as a multiple of the 1/sqrt(N) noise
floor:

| | status-2 count | barbed-end banding | **myosin banding** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| k_mm = 0 | 75 | 1.78 | 2.07 |
| k_mm = 50 | 18 | 2.59 | **16.34** |
| k_mm = 200 | 9 | 2.52 | **17.72** |

**The M-band works** — it registers thick-filament centres into planes at 16–18x
the noise floor. But it costs ACTN2 maturation badly (status-2 75 → 18 → 9) and
`k_mm = 200` **fails the health gate** (myosin force-cap fraction 0.0698,
clamp-dominated). Total ACTN2 links rise (0.21 → 0.37 → 0.52) while status-2
collapses: many encounter complexes, no maturation. Needs a much lower k_mm
scan (try 5–20) and a look at why maturation is suppressed.

### Motor validation rigs (`motor_validation` target)

| rig | overlap | velocity | vs diffusion | expected | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| free | 0.845 | −2.22 um/s | 1.60 | translocate | PASS |
| linked | 0.391 | −1.73 um/s | 1.25 | slower, directed | PASS |
| balanced | 1.000 | +3.45 um/s | 2.49 | settle, ~0 drift | **FAIL** |

Free and linked confirm **the heads are motors**: ~30–45% of the 5 um/s unloaded
velocity, correct direction, slower under elastic load.

`balanced` does not reproduce its own gate (sandbox reports terminal drift
0.003–0.008 um/s; this rig gives +3.45 um/s, ~500x off). Two known differences
remain unported: the sandbox runs a **30 ms fixed-geometry pre-equilibration**
before releasing dynamics, and drives the cell through `sim.set_axial_cell(...)`
which this rig never calls. **Do not trust the balanced row until those are
ported.**

Known rig defect: the activation arguments have no effect — floor 0/frac 0.5
gives bit-identical output to floor 1/frac 1, so `set_am_thin_activation` needs
an explicit call that is missing. Does not affect free/linked.

## Corrections to earlier conclusions

Recorded because each was stated with more confidence than the evidence
supported, and each cost time.

- **The sliding throttle is not the missing setting.** The minimal cell does not
  set `am_overlap_target_ratio` either. Direct test: 0.25 and 0.40 changed
  nothing (drift 2.80/3.13 vs 3.13 baseline).
- **Sliding speed is not the root cause** — head occupancy is. The heads-only
  run has 8x the drift of activation-only yet a healthier network.
- **`crosslinkable_ratio` is the operative mechanism after all**, but driven by
  head occupancy, not by sliding sweeping links off sites.
- **There is no maturation/survival trade-off.** That appeared only because it
  was measured at 20 ms, before maturation begins.
- **"Filaments are being drawn deeper into the myosins" was wrong.**
  `am_overlap_available` is summed over every myosin an actin touches, so it
  grew because engagement count grew 71 → 1848 pairs, while per-pair overlap
  *decreased*.
- **"The heads are not motors" was wrong.** It came from three stacked bugs in
  my own rig — reversed actin polarity (the productive-half rule correctly
  refuses it), `fix_myosin=1` excluding the only myosin from the interaction
  loops, and `set_periodicity()` called before placement so the neighbour list
  was built on the constructor's random positions.
- **"Sign-blind detachment removes the ratchet" was wrong.**
  `active_crossbridge_force` with `bidirectional=false` returns exactly zero for
  backward strain, so rectification is in the force law and does not depend on
  detachment at all.

## Open questions

1. **Z-disc banding is absent in every configuration.** Barbed-end Fourier
   amplitude sits at the noise floor (1.6–2.6x, p ~ 0.4–0.6). The M-band now
   bands the myosins strongly; the thin filaments still do not follow.
2. **Polar order is zero.** P = 0.02–0.04 against a 1/sqrt(N) = 0.040 random
   baseline in every run, while S reaches 0.77–0.93. Filaments are aligned but
   point randomly along the axis. Nothing in the model sorts polarity — the
   alignment tether is explicitly nematic. This is the likeliest reason banding
   and net transport are both absent at scale.
3. **Why does myomesin suppress ACTN2 maturation?** Needs a low-k_mm scan.
4. **Port the pre-equilibration and axial-cell setup into the balanced rig**
   before treating the three-way motor check as complete.
5. **A-M overlap creep** — per-pair |s| drifts inward late in long runs
   (−1.13 um/s at 193 ms). At diffusion-level net displacement this is probably
   geometric repositioning, but it has not been cleanly separated.

## Operational notes

- **One partition per job.** Multi-partition requests (`--partition=a,b,c`) are
  rejected with `BadConstraints` on this cluster. Cost time twice.
- 8 cores gives ~18 steps/s at 640 actins, ~9 at 1,280. 32 cores gives ~1.9x
  (not the ~2.5x extrapolated from the 12.9x-at-32-threads figure, which was
  measured against single-thread).
- Analysis on a dev node: `srun -p dev -c 2 --mem=6G`. Larger memory requests
  hit `QOSMaxMemoryPerUser` while 32 GB simulation jobs are running.
- Read in-progress trajectories with `locking=False`; the file tracks the writer
  to within ~1 frame.
