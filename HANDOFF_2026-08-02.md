## Executive summary

The scientific target is an active, Brownian, three-dimensional sarcomere
assembly model in which:

1. turnover-capable ACTN2 anneals antiparallel actin barbed ends toward a
   preferred A-A overlap of approximately `0.10 um`;
2. myosin heads slide actin and generate overlap-dependent active force;
3. individual, explicit titin-like molecules bind dynamically and are exactly
   slack in compression;
4. the combined system develops a finite A-M overlap instead of walking actin
   through the myosin bare zone; and
5. hexagonal order is emergent. There is no pre-existing Z node, six-actin
   centroid, ring-centroid force, or imposed Z-disc lattice.

The minimal 2-myosin/4-actin cell now passes a one-second, four-seed stability
gate with the relaxed ACTN2 and explicit-titin mechanism. That mechanism was
ported into the large production driver and a guarded 1,280-actin pilot was
submitted as Slurm job `37226225`.

The large pilot was numerically healthy but scientifically failed: all 1,280
actins were titin-bound by the first analyzed 7 ms checkpoint, even though
there were only 115 live ACTN2 links. The run was cancelled at 9 ms of
committed trajectory (`18,000` steps; `dt = 0.5 us`) rather than extended.

The next change should address titin capture specificity, not tune toward an
arbitrary target percentage. The recommended physical rule is that a *new*
titin-actin association requires the individual actin barbed end to be part
of a live ACTN2 A-A link. This is local evidence of a nascent Z-disc complex;
it does not require a six-actin centroid or a pre-existing Z disc. Once titin
is bound, it should survive ordinary ACTN2 turnover, and the already
implemented short same-site rebinding memory should remain available.

## Current scientific question

Can myosin motors, turnover-capable ACTN2, and explicit tension-only titin-like
molecules organize a stable sarcomere-like arrangement with both:

- a preferred A-A overlap near `0.10 um`; and
- a finite, stable A-M overlap before the myosin bare zone?

The model is an active nonequilibrium system. It is related to stable-overlap
models for antiparallel microtubules, but it is not merely reproducing those
models: it has explicit bipolar myosin head cycles, filament translation and
rotation, torque, ACTN2 turnover, and a 3-D filament lattice.

## Model decisions that should not be reverted

### No artificial Z-disc scaffold

Do not restore any of the following deprecated sandbox devices:

- a force between a six-actin centroid and a myosin tip;
- a ring-centroid spring;
- a radial ring-shape energy;
- an imposed Z node;
- an imposed six-actin azimuthal template; or
- a preassigned actin-to-myosin ownership map for the large assembly.

Titin must act through explicit material domains attached to individual
filaments. ACTN2 must act through explicit sparse A-A links.

### Explicit ACTN2

The production model uses sparse, local, turnover-capable ACTN2 links rather
than a single pair-level spring. Current promoted parameters are:

- barbed-end association zone: `0.20 um`;
- weak/status-1 connector capture distance: `0.10 um` total centerline reach;
- weak/status-1 radial stiffness: `75 pN/um` per molecule;
- locked/status-2 preferred centerline distance: `0.055 um`;
- preferred A-A axial overlap: `0.10 um`;
- explicit contour/rupture length: `0.12 um`;
- local site exclusion: `0.0192 um`;
- maximum distinct A-A neighbors per actin: `2` in the current pilot; and
- status mode: `productive_register`.

Status 1 is a soft encounter complex. Status 2 retains the locked-link radial
stiffness. The state-dependent radial stiffness must be used consistently in
mechanics, load calculation, diagnostics, and saved metadata.

### Explicit titin

Each titin is a material domain anchored to a particular myosin half and
azimuthal track. It binds one individual actin barbed end. The large resolved
lattice permits at most one titin owner per actin. The minimal four-rod model
temporarily allowed up to three titins per coarse actin only because one
coarse rod represented a three-actin bundle; that multivalency must not be
copied into the resolved 1,280-actin model.

Current promoted titin parameters are:

- three material domains per myosin half;
- tension-only flat-bottom force law;
- stiffness: `40 pN/um`;
- slack length: `0.750 um`;
- candidate search distance: `0.850 um`;
- zero-strain association rate: `5000 /s`;
- zero-strain dissociation rate: `0.5 /s`;
- elastic transition-state fraction: `theta = 1.0`;
- same-material-partner rebinding memory: `0.020 s`; and
- same-partner rate multiplier during that window: `100`.

The force is exactly zero for end-to-end distance less than or equal to the
slack length. Association remains strain gated. Titin forces are reciprocal
and the off-center anchors produce physical filament torque.

## Root cause of 100% titin occupancy

The saturation follows directly from the current rules:

1. There are `3840` titin domains for `1280` actins, so molecular capacity is
   three times the one-owner-per-actin cap.
2. Every unbound titin searches for a compatible barbed end inside a very
   large `0.85 um` radius.
3. The tension-only elastic energy is exactly zero throughout the entire
   `distance <= 0.75 um` slack region.
4. Therefore all candidates inside the slack region receive the full
   `5000 /s` association rate.
5. The corresponding zero-strain binding time is only `0.2 ms`, whereas the
   zero-strain bound lifetime is `2 s` (`k_off = 0.5 /s`).
6. In the dense random production box, essentially every actin quickly has at
   least one compatible titin domain. The monovalent ownership cap then fills
   deterministically to 1,280/1,280.

Changing `theta` to 1 did not solve this because elastic gating cannot
distinguish candidates inside a force-free slack region. Increasing slack and
capture reach solved the preassembled minimal geometry but made nonspecific
large-system capture easier.

## Recommended next implementation

Add an explicit, optional capture gate such as:

`--titin_require_live_actn2=true`

For an ordinary new association, `_best_explicit_titin_candidate()` should
reject actin `a` unless `aa_links_by_actin[a]` contains at least one active
local ACTN2 molecule. Status 1 should be sufficient; requiring status 2 would
recreate the original low-capture problem and would improperly make titin
depend on mature motor load.

Important semantics:

1. This gate applies only to *formation* of a new titin-actin link.
2. A titin that is already bound does not detach merely because ACTN2 turns
   over.
3. During the 20-ms molecular rebinding window, the same titin may recapture
   its previous actin by identity and distance even if the ACTN2 molecule is
   momentarily absent. This permits annealing rather than brittle coupling.
4. After that window, a global new-partner search again requires a live ACTN2
   link.
5. Do not use myosin-head occupancy as the gate. The titin molecule remains a
   distinct physical binder rather than an occupancy-triggered spring.
6. Do not introduce a centroid, Z node, or preassigned lattice.

This creates a local sequence:

`ACTN2 barbed-end encounter -> titin capture -> ACTN2 turnover/annealing`

without assuming that a complete Z disc already exists.

- an isolated actin inside the titin capture shell cannot bind when the

- the same actin can bind after a live status-1 ACTN2 link is added;
- status 2 is not required;
- loss of the ACTN2 link does not forcibly release an already bound titin;
- same-partner rebinding works during the 20-ms memory window; and
- after the memory expires, a new association again requires live ACTN2.

Run the complete test suite with:

`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 HDF5_USE_FILE_LOCKING=FALSE ctest --test-dir <build> --output-on-failure`

### Small and medium occupancy diagnostics

Do not jump directly from 4 actins to 1,280. Use the same density and ratios:

1. A small stochastic assembly, for example `128 actins / 64 myosins`, for at
   least 20 ms and multiple seeds.
2. A medium assembly, for example `320 actins / 160 myosins`, for at least
   20 ms and two seeds.

For each frame, record:

- fraction of actins in any live ACTN2 link;
- fraction in status 1 and status 2 separately;
- titin-bound fraction;
- fraction of titin-bound actins currently supported by ACTN2;
- number of new titin associations whose actin had live ACTN2 support;
- titin association, dissociation, and same-site rebinding counts;
- weak and strong motor-head counts;
- force/torque cap fractions;
- ACTN2 contour ruptures; and
- A-A and A-M overlap distributions.

The gate should be structural rather than an arbitrary occupancy target:

- no unsupported ordinary titin association events;
- titin occupancy rises as ACTN2-supported barbed-end structures form, rather
  than jumping immediately to 100%;
- stochastic titin turnover remains visible;
- status-1 ACTN2 is common enough to nucleate capture;
- all geometry remains finite;
- numerical caps remain below the existing threshold; and
- no systematic ACTN2 contour rupture occurs.
