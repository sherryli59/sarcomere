## 3. Code changes made (all in the production tree)

Binary: `cpp/build_myosin_spacing_20260807/sarcomere`, sha256 `4abe71ca4e4c...`.
Source backups: `cpp_src_backup_prespacing_0634`, `cpp_include_backup_prespacing_0634`.

1. **`--myosin_exclusion_distance`** — decouples the myosin-myosin exclusion
   *range* from the myosin radius (was hardcoded `2*myosin.radius`). Set to
   `0.055*sqrt(3) = 0.0953` to hold the thick-filament lattice at the spacing that
   seats an actin in the trigonal interstice. Raising `myosin_radius` instead is
   circular — it pushes the AM preferred centreline out and the target runs away.

2. **Data race fix (important).** `_record_am_forced_rupture` and
   `_record_am_thick_transition` appended to *shared* member vectors from inside
   the `omp for` in `_process_dynamic_am_heads`, with no per-thread buffer and no
   critical section. Concurrent `std::vector::insert` corrupted the heap — this
   killed cell D (SIGSEGV in production, SIGABRT under gdb, both in
   `_M_range_insert<double const*>`). Now guarded by named critical sections
   matching the file's existing pattern. **This bug predates all of my work and
   could silently corrupt any multi-threaded run in which two ruptures coincide.**

3. **M-band transverse gate** now tracks the myosin spacing:
   `max(2*am_cutoff, exclusion + am_cutoff)`. Previously fixed at `2*am_cutoff` =
   0.10, only 5 nm above a 0.0953 lattice, so fluctuation would switch the M-band
   spring off intermittently. Inert when the exclusion is unset.

4. **`--am_stroke_detailed_balance`** (+ `am_stroke_base_rate`, `am_stroke_theta`,
   `am_stroke_free_energy`) — discrete working stroke with strain-dependent
   forward/reverse rates satisfying detailed balance, replacing the prescribed
   `v = v0(1-F/F_stall)` ramp. Force-velocity becomes emergent. **Untested in
   production**; only exercised in the single-AM rig.

All four verified **bit-identical to the frozen binary at defaults**, at
`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` (0/2400 components differ). 28/28 ctest pass.

## 4. Established facts (with evidence)

**Numerics**
- The model is **not deterministic** under OpenMP. Same binary, same seed, 16
  threads, twice -> max |dx| = 0.208 um, all 2400 components differ, in 20 ms.
  Verify code changes at 1 thread; judge arms on aggregates with replicate seeds.
- **Force cap = 26.373 pN for both species**, computed from the *myosin* mobility
  (`compute_max_force` uses one D for both). Fires on 29.6% of myosin-steps; 44%
  of myosins pinned at it. `myosin/force` is **post-cap** — do not read forces
  from it. Use `myosin/am_tension_minus/plus`, which are pre-cap.
- Mobilities (printed at startup): beta = 233.644 /(pN.um),
  beta*D_actin = **70.2**, beta*D_myosin = **19.0** um/(pN.s).

**Force laws**
- `compute_myosin_repulsion` normalises overlap by the cutoff; actin-actin and
  actin-myosin do not. So MM at nominal k=1000 is *effectively* 20000 pN/um while
  AM at k=1000 is 1000 — a 20x asymmetry at equal nominal stiffness.
- AM contact: 16.6% of bound pairs sit inside the 0.040 um hard core; 59% of
  actins have their nearest myosin inside it. MM penetration is ~0.2 nm.

**Motors**
- 18 heads/myosin (9 per half), 4.5 pN stall each -> 40.5 pN per half, 81 pN total.
- Actual: 5.2 of 18 strong, ~1 pN each. **~5% of capacity.**
- `am_stall_force` is both a hard clamp on the head spring *and* the stall term in
  `working_stroke_velocity`, so it is a genuine stall force.
- **A free filament outruns the stroke at ~0.25 pN.** Total motor force on an
  unanchored thin filament saturates at `v0/(beta*D)` regardless of head count or
  stall force. Raising `am_stall_force` or head count cannot raise force while thin
  filaments are unanchored — relevant to the HCM plan.
- Strong heads are **tension-only** by default. An overrun head exerts exactly
  zero force *and* never gets `axial_energy` set, so it also gets no
  strain-accelerated detachment — an inert placeholder occupying a site.
  `--am_bidirectional_crossbridge` fixes both.
- The **passive AM tether acts only through bound heads**. There is no attraction
  between unattached filaments; association is maintained purely by crowding.

**Structure (baseline)**
- Z-disc forms at lambda = 1.608 um (Lx/3), order 0.67, saturating ~2750 ms.
- M-band lags badly (0.24 at 5000 ms, still rising).
- Titin: 42% slot occupancy, **75.6% of live links slack** (tension-only, shorter
  than rest). Only 28-40 of 320 myosins bilaterally taut — but those reach M-band
  order **0.678** vs 0.319 for the rest, so the mechanism works, it is just starved.
- cb2 actins: A-A overlap median **10.3%** of actin length (exactly the 0.10 um
  target — this part of the model works); A-M burial **100%** at every percentile.
- Myosin polarity: only 23.6% engage balanced numbers of +/-x cb2 actins, 27.1%
  engage a single polarity — yet polarity imbalance does not correlate with net
  axial drive (r = +0.088). Heads cancel within each half.

## 5. Open questions

1. **Replicate D.** Single seed. Needs >=12 seeds per the arrest-rig bimodality result.
2. **Extend D** past 5000 ms — ACTN2 had not plateaued.
3. **Why does ACTN2 die in C?** Actins are *not* expelled (9.3 myosins in reach,
   5.25 heads/actin — more than baseline; status-1 healthy at 298). Only promotion
   to status-2 fails, pointing at the `productive_register` criterion. But D rescues
   it, so the story is not purely support starvation — worth understanding, because
   it is the mechanism by which the transverse term matters.
4. **Why is the exclusion invisible in the MM nearest-neighbour median?** All four
   arms sit at 0.0498-0.0500 even though C and D differ only in that flag and differ
   enormously in outcome. Coordination does drop (11.05 -> 7.74). Job 38793833
   (crossbridges off) was meant to test whether the feature works at all; D has
   already shown it does, so 38793833 is now only diagnostic.
5. **titin arm** — timed out at 4370 ms, only analysed to 3050 ms. At 3050 ms it
   was strictly worse than baseline (Z 0.190 vs 0.655) and the slack fraction
   barely moved (74.6% -> 68.9%) because attachment distance *scales with* rest
   length. Probably a dead end; see section 6.
6. **Detailed-balance stroke** untested in production.

## 6. Corrections — claims I made and then falsified

Recorded because several were reported confidently before being checked.

- "64% of bound AM pairs beyond rupture distance" — **false**, 0%. My
  segment-segment routine degenerated for near-parallel rods.
- "beta*D_actin = 19" — **false**, 70.2. I inferred it from the force ceiling, but
  the ceiling uses the myosin mobility for both species.
- "`am_stall_force` has no velocity dependence" — **false**; it appears in
  `working_stroke_velocity`.
- Titin commensurability argument (rest length 0.665 => 2.83 um sarcomere
  incompatible with the box) — **built on the assumption that myosins sit centred
  between Z-discs, which they do not** (M-band was 0.215). Predicted titin span
  0.054 um; measured 0.541 um, in data I already had.
- "Cell D's 9x slowdown is the extended exclusion range" — **false**; node
  contention. Cell D at the same exclusion ran at 0.055 s/step.
- "Detailed balance cuts M-line penetration 60%" — **artifact**; the actin was
  escaping transversely in the first single-AM rig.
- "Cell B shows transverse spacing does nothing / the feature may be broken" —
  **wrong**; D proves the feature has a large effect.
- The `--dependency=afterok` equivalence gate could never have passed for *any*
  patch, because it compared 16-thread runs of a nondeterministic model.

## 7. Instruments and pitfalls

  meaningful bit-comparison. `/scratch/users/xsli/sarcomere/spacing_check/determinism.sh`.
