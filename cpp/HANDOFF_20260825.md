## 1. Current scientific direction

- Myomesin is disabled (`k_mm=0`). There is no explicit M-band in the present
  model, so using myomesin to align every nearby myosin was both too strong and
  conceptually inconsistent with the intended system.
- The current axial box is `Lx=6 um`, with `Ly=Lz=2 um`. This was chosen to make
  an `n=3` axial organization geometrically available while disfavoring `n=4`,
  and to leave room between neighboring myosin groups for Z-disc-like actin
  organization.
- Titin was not used to enforce that spacing. The explicit titin term is
  tension-only: shortening a unit below its rest length does not supply the
  missing repulsion.
- The current density test compares `A1600/M320` and `A1600/M640`. It was
  launched because the earlier ACTN2 network did not percolate across the box,
  and insufficient actin/myosin density was a plausible contributor.

Do not interpret the `M-band order` diagnostic as evidence that an M-band force
exists. It is only an axial myosin-order observable in these `k_mm=0` runs.

## 3. Why A1600/M320 was so slow

The slowdown was algorithmic, not HDF5 output and not a stalled node.

Each bilateral ACTN2 link formed the union of the two actins' frozen myosin
neighbor lists. The neighbor cutoff must cover long rods and skin distance, so
the union is much larger than the actual ACTN2--myosin contact region. The
hard-tip Gauss--Seidel solver and force evaluation then ran expensive
narrow-phase geometry on nearly every member of that broad union.

The first principled sparse-incidence profile (`40737986`) completed 20,000
steps in 54m23s and showed:

- 10.466 billion potential tip constraints;
- 11.348 billion narrow-phase solve evaluations;
- only 311,830 actual corrections (0.00275% of evaluations);
- 1,295.1 s in ACTN2-tip projection;
- 1,675.8 s in `update_system`;
- negligible output cost.

Sparse incidence fixed repeated Gauss--Seidel rescans after a correction, but
it still built and initially visited the enormous full candidate universe.

## 4. Implemented fix: exact conservative spatial broad phase

The working tree now uses cheap, conservative spatial bounds before all
expensive ACTN2--myosin body and tip geometry.

This is the production default. The production integrator calls
`spatial_incidence` explicitly, the executable announces the traversal at
startup, and HDF5 metadata records
`aa_zdisc_tip_projection_traversal=spatial_incidence_exact_conservative_broadphase`.
The sparse-incidence and full-scan traversals remain available only to the
parity/performance fixture; they are not production command-line modes.

For a tip contact, a narrow-phase contact is possible only if the periodic
distance from the ACTN2-patch midpoint to the myosin center is within

`sqrt((myosin_length/2 + tip_onset)^2 + transverse_cutoff^2)`.

For a body/connector contact, the conservative reach is

`patch_half_length + myosin_length/2 + outer_contact_distance`.

These are enclosing-sphere tests. A rejected pair is geometrically incapable
of satisfying the narrow-phase contact law; this is not a heuristic cutoff and
does not change the force law.

The projection solver also avoids materializing two full constraint records
per link/myosin pair. It caches compact link/pair neighbor unions and reverse
incidence. Initial candidates are stored in canonical order. After any
projection:

1. all links incident to either moved actin are spatially rediscovered;
2. if the myosin is movable, all links incident to that myosin are rediscovered;
3. constraints later in canonical Gauss--Seidel order enter the current sweep;
4. earlier or equal constraints enter the next sweep.

This dynamic discovery is required for correctness because repairing one
contact can bring another contact into range. A zero-correction sweep remains
a complete convergence certificate.

Important numerical behavior is unchanged:

- solver tolerance remains `1e-9 um` (`0.001 pm`);
- maximum sweeps remains 64;
- the post-solve audit tolerance is `1e-6 um` (`1 pm`);
- an audit violation logs a warning and continues instead of terminating the
  trajectory.

The intended viewer semantics are:

- all actins use the same base color;
- controls independently show/hide all actin, status-1 actin, status-2 actin,
  and myosin;
- ACTN2 and myomesin have distinct colors;

  from spatial proximity. The earlier screenshot with apparently floating
