# Handoff — 2026-10-21
## The barbed-tip endstop and the ACTN2 burial deletion rule

Scope: why ACTN2 crosslinks die of "burial", whether the soft repulsion force works, and
what in that mechanism is actually defective. Trees: `zdisclink_20261020` (code),
`burialzone_20261020` (rigs), `s1k1000_prod_20261008` (production reference).

---

## 1. Headline

**The repulsion force works and is correctly implemented. The defect is the deletion rule.**
Of three parameter fixes proposed for it, a factorial says **only one helps** — and the other
two make things worse, including one that was my central criticism of the design.

## 2. Established findings

### The force itself
| claim | evidence |
|---|---|
| Implements its documented law exactly | \|F − F_max·tanh(kχ/F_max)\| max **5.3e-15 pN** over 4,930 samples |
| Reaches full strength | max **20.00 pN** of a 20 pN ceiling |
| Is genuinely load-bearing | paired 6-seed A/B: buried fraction **0.006% ON vs 11.70% OFF** (+11.70 ± 4.55 SE); max penetration 18.6 → 245.6 nm; per-seed OFF reaches 399/313/654 nm |
| Is purely **axial** | `force_on_actin = force * end_sign * myosin_axis` (`interaction.cpp:1233`) |
| Ejects a tip placed inside | push-out rig: deepest at ~5 steps, **outside the capsule by step 20 (~150 µs)**, then settles at +50 nm (the onset edge, where force → 0) and penetration stays 0 for 12,000 steps |

### The deletion rule
| claim | evidence |
|---|---|
| Probability-1 veto at 10 nm | `decide_barbed_tip_capsule_penetration` sets `probability = 1.0`; `aa_occlusion_rupture_penetration = 0.010`, `aa_overlap_occlusion_veto = 1` in all production |
| Dominant death mode | **65.3% of 5,963,901** production removals (by arm: kaa0/kaa3 12-16%, hph27+msat6 52.6%, hph54+msat6 60-72%, **hph54+msat0 78.6-83.1%**) |
| Fires on non-contact | distance tip→myosin **rod**: only **3 of 44 (6.8%)** are genuine surface overlaps; 93% have the tip entirely outside, median gap +12.7 nm |
| Fires beyond the filament | **81.8%** of flagged tips are axially **past the filament end**, inside the capsule's rounded cap bulge (~44 nm) |
| Threshold below the actin radius | kill 10 nm < `actin_radius` 15 nm |
| Burial itself is transient | per-step: episodes past the kill depth last **median 2 steps = 20 µs**, max 320 µs; exit/entry ratio **1.00**; zero actins permanently stuck |

### Geometry
- Three independent parameters coincide at exactly **80 nm**: `d_am` resting spacing, head capture
  distance (measured, median 80.0 nm over 4,940 bound head-frames), and the endstop gate radius
  `aa_occlusion_capture_radius`. Consequence: **~1000 gate open/shut cycles per actin per second**
  on perfectly healthy filaments.
- Head anchors sit on the **filament axis** (`_am_head_anchor_radius()` returns 0 when
  `am_head_azimuth=0`, which is all production). 3 crowns per half at |s| = 188/413/638 nm.
- Head capture is **a transverse plane per crown** the actin must cross, not a sphere — a head is
  only a candidate when its anchor **projects onto the actin segment** (`sarcomere.cpp:3418`),
  true in 100% of bound head-frames. A 1 µm actin crosses all three; no axial gaps.
- Burial requires **disorder**. In a registered `two_sarcomere_unit` lattice at hph=27: penetration
  on arrival median **0.0 nm** (production: 347.6 nm), 0.81% past kill on arrival (production: 100%),
  and **zero burial deaths across 12 runs**.

## 3. The Tier-1 factorial (job 48907904, 16 runs, complete)

onset × kill-threshold × gate-radius, 2 seeds, push-out rig (shift 0.205), hph=27.

| 0.050 | **0.050** | 0.080 | 3.505 | 7.00 | 16.2% | **0.0** | **0.0** | **← BEST** |
| 0.002 | 0.010 | 0.080 | 3.381 | 4.91 | 30.1% | 43.1 | 4.5 |
| 0.002 | 0.050 | 0.080 | 3.433 | 7.00 | 7.9% | 14.1 | 0.0 |
| 0.050 | 0.010 | 0.130 | 3.245 | 4.89 | 47.5% | 0.0 | 3.0 |
| 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.130 | 3.245 | 4.89 | 47.5% | 0.0 | 3.0 |
| 0.002 | 0.010 | 0.130 | 3.380 | 4.89 | 48.8% | 23.9 | 3.0 |
| 0.002 | 0.050 | 0.130 | 3.380 | 4.89 | 48.8% | 23.9 | 3.0 |

**Conclusions:**
1. **Raise `aa_occlusion_rupture_penetration` 0.010 → 0.050.** Burial deaths 1.5 → **0.0** at
   essentially no cost (strong −0.4%, status-2 unchanged at 7.00). **The one fix that works.**
2. **Do NOT narrow `aa_zdisc_tip_onset`.** At 0.002, maxPen goes 0.0 → **43.1 nm**, burial +3.0,
   status-2 −29.9%. The wide onset is *why* tips never penetrate — the force acting early is the
   mechanism, not waste. **This refutes my own central criticism** that 99.89% of the force being
   spent outside the capsule is a defect.
3. **Do NOT widen `aa_occlusion_capture_radius` to 0.130.** Costs strong heads −7.8% and
   status-2 −30.2%, and makes the force fire on ~48% of steps. Consistent across all four cells.

Caveat: n=2 seeds, burial counts 0–4.5. The status-2 effects (7.00 vs 4.89) are clean and
consistent; the burial-count differences are suggestive only.

### The A-band rule — STATUS REVERSED, now OPEN

Your physical argument: a Z-disc crosslink overlapping a thick filament's axial span is not a
valid sarcomere, contact or not. Three tests, the first two of which I got wrong:

**(i) Radial-window enrichment — INVALID.** Scanned 80-800 nm, found enrichment ~1.0 everywhere.
The test was incoherent: the A-band is a **slab**, so conditioning on lateral proximity is
meaningless.

**(ii) Standing population, slab metric — CIRCULAR.** Live crosslinks sit at **0.13x** the A-band
coverage of a random point (median 0.0375 vs 0.2938, n=127,073). I concluded "the lattice already
excludes them, nothing to build". **That is survivorship bias**: the veto is deleting 65% of
crosslinks in the same run, so a depleted surviving population is exactly what an effective veto
would produce.

**(iii) Slab-metric enrichment, settled half only — the veto IS selecting for it.**
Both earlier errors compounded: the events had been sampled over the whole run (including the
disordered first half) and compared against settled-half survivors. Corrected:

| population | median A-band coverage |
|---|---|
| random points | 0.2938 |
| **burial deletions** | **0.1062** |
| link formations | 0.0719 |
| geometric deaths | 0.0688 |
| stochastic deaths | 0.0563 |
| live links (standing) | 0.0375 |

**Burial deletions carry 1.48x the A-band coverage of newly formed links** (mean 1.35x), and are
the ONLY death mode enriched -- stochastic runs *below* formations (0.78x), geometric matches.
So the existing rule is already acting as a crude, indirect A-band filter.

**(iv) Cross-arm correlation — CONFOUNDED.** r(burial share, standing coverage) = **-0.476** over
18 arms, pointing the way "veto excludes" predicts. But the split is categorical: the low-burial
arms are the disordered `kaa0`/`kaa3` baselines, where live coverage ~= random (0.23 vs 0.25) and
there is no exclusion at all; every organised arm has both more burial and more exclusion. Arms
differ in the parameters driving both quantities, so this cannot separate the hypotheses.

**DECISIVE TEST RUNNING: job 48934005**, `vetoab_20261021`. Production config (kaa3, sigma 0.40,
hph27, msat6), `--aa_overlap_occlusion_veto` true vs false, paired on 2 seeds, 1000 ms, ~3 h each.
Measures whether A-band-occupying crosslinks **accumulate** when nothing removes them. This is the
only test immune to survivorship, and it should have been run before any conclusion was drawn.

**Do not act on the A-band question until that lands.** Specifically, do not repeat the earlier
claim that the rule is "ruled out".

## 5. Code changes (all default-inert, 42/42 tests pass)

In `zdisclink_20261020`:
- **`--minimal_catch_myosin_inward_shift`** (µm, sentinel −1e6 = variant default). Slides both
  thick filaments toward the central Z-disc. Works for `minimal_catch_balanced*` **and**
  `two_sarcomere_unit`. For the latter, central-tip clearance = **(0.20 − shift) µm**, verified.
- **`--aa_occlusion_rupture_dwell`** (seconds, default 0). Grace period the burial must persist
  before deletion. Per-link counter `AACrosslink::occlusion_dwell`, reset when the tip clears.
  Not persisted across resume (documented). **Verified bitwise inert**: 0 of 244 datasets differ
  vs the pre-patch binary, NaN-aware and raw-byte.
  Effect at hph=9: TIP_BURIAL **21 → 0**, but total deaths only 150 → 140 (substitution) and
  link-count gains not significant at n=6.
- **`--range FIRST LAST`** added to `build-minimal-wrap-animation.py` (frame window before
  striding). Default path still produces exactly 121 frames.

## 6. Traps found — read before trusting any instrument here

1. **`actin/velocity` is all zeros** in every run. Reads as valid data. Test for all-zero first.
2. **Recorded penetration is never filled when the endstop is off** (`aa_zdisc_tip_contact=false`
   skips the whole block). Comparing that field across an ON/OFF A/B reports "no penetration
   without the endstop" — the exact inverse of the truth. Derive geometrically for both arms.
3. **`recorded[t]` matches geometry at `t−1`.** The diagnostic is written during force evaluation,
   before the position update. At lag 1 the error is **0.0000 nm** median *and* max.
4. **Two different `capture_reach` values**: the gate at `sarcomere.cpp:3431` uses `am_cutoff`
   (50 nm); the logged diagnostic at 3764 uses `am_surface_gap + am_cutoff` (65 nm). This
   discrepancy produced three wrong answers in a row. **Measure, don't read parameter names.**
5. **NaN breaks `np.array_equal`.** `aa_zdisc_tip_gap/rho` hold NaN when the gate is shut, so a
   bitwise A/B reports false differences. Use `equal_nan=True` *and* a raw-byte check.
6. **16 threads on a 4-actin rig runs 6.7× slower** than 2 threads (pure barrier contention).
   Always pin `OMP_NUM_THREADS` on minimal rigs.
7. **Browser pane caps at 512 KB.** A 745 KB viewer silently fails to open.
8. **Minimal rigs are under-powered**: `am_heads_per_half=9` vs production 27 → 0.878 vs 3.568
   strong heads per actin, 88.6% vs ~4% chance some actin is unengaged, and one of four actins
   detaches at 78 ms and never returns. Check mean strong heads, P(zero strong) and per-actin
   distance-to-axis before trusting any minimal-rig rate.
9. **Use the canonical visualization pipeline.** `VISUALIZATION_PIPELINE.md` says copy the builder
   and template and change only the run table/title/output. Four hand-written templates in this
   session re-hit problems the canonical one solved months ago. `build-minimal-wrap-animation.py`
   already does trajectory playback with motor-head layers and a validator (110 checks).

## 7. Open questions

- **Endstop head cost** — significant at hph=9, untested at hph=27 (the mechanism never fired).
  To test, perturb the start (`minimal_catch_tilt_deg`, or `three_membered_lattice` jitter) so
  tips approach at production density.
- **Grace period benefit** — burial deaths go to zero but total deaths barely move. Needs a
  production A/B, where burial is 65% of deaths rather than 14%.
- **The right deletion criterion.** A-band is out. Remaining candidates: surface contact
  (tip-to-rod < 65 nm, fires on 6.8%) or a rate-based veto so marginal geometry costs bond
  lifetime instead of killing it. The factorial's kill=0.050 result is a crude version of the latter.
- **The 80 nm triple coincidence** is still the root geometric oddity and the factorial says you
  cannot fix it by moving the gate.
