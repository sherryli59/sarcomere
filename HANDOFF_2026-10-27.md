# Handoff — 2026-10-27: ACTN2 overstretch and the Z-disc hard projection

## Goal (decided)

**No hard projection overstretching the crosslinks.** The Z-disc tip *hard projection*
moves actins directly, outside the equation of motion, and stretches their ACTN2 links until they
rupture. It should be removed. A **soft repulsion plus a deletion rule** should keep thick
filaments and Z-discs apart instead.

Production (`burialzone_20261020/prodargs.sh`) still runs with the projection **on**. The flag
`aa_zdisc_tip_hard_projection` is not set there, so it takes its default, `true`. The only run
with it off is the test arm below.

## 1. The confusion, so it isn't repeated

There are five Z-disc / myosin-end mechanisms in the code. Three were repeatedly mixed up.

| mechanism | point it tests | radius around the myosin axis | what it does | code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. soft patch tip force | ACTN2 patch = midpoint of the two linked actins' binding sites | 130 nm (flat to 100 nm) | soft force, onset 50 nm, 1000 pN/µm, max 20 pN | `interaction.cpp:1185` `compute_zdisc_myosin_tip_contact` |
| B. **hard projection** | same patch | 130 nm | position correction: moves **both actins of that link** along the myosin's outward axis and the myosin back | `langevin.cpp:361` `resolve_actn2_tip_hard_contacts` |
| C. bare-tip endstop | each actin's barbed tip | 80 nm capsule | soft force; gated on a live ACTN2 link when `aa_zdisc_tip_contact_requires_link=true` | `interaction.cpp:1265` `compute_barbed_tip_myosin_endstop` |
| D. burial deletion (reason 3) | each linked actin's barbed tip | 80 nm capsule (`aa_occlusion_capture_radius`) | deletes the link when the tip is ≥ 50 nm past the capsule boundary | `sarcomere.cpp` ~1800–2005; `catch_bond_kinetics.h:84` |
| E. collision deletion (reason 4) | ACTN2 connector vs thick-filament **body** | radial hard core | deletes on radial overlap only | `interaction.cpp:1028` `compute_actn2_myosin_contact` |

**Confusion 1: "we agreed to disable the projection".** On 10-20 the user agreed that **C** (the
bare-tip endstop) is redundant. That is why `aa_zdisc_tip_contact_requires_link=true` is now the
standing default. The user also asked for a flag to remove **B**, with the rule "if the crosslink
enters the myosin, it's dead". The flag was built (`main.cpp:518`, default true, verified inert)
but never used: switching it off sent reason 4 to 0%. I then claimed, *without measuring it*,
that crosslinks would sit inside the thick filament.

**Confusion 2: "patches sink into myosins".** That is wrong. With the projection off, nothing
enters a thick filament:
- The "penetrating" patches sit **67–130 nm from the myosin axis (median 117 nm)**. The myosin
  radius is 50 nm, so they are beside it in the lattice.
- The recorded negative gap is the **myosin tip overshooting the Z-disc patch along its own
  axis**. So B is an **axial end stop for thick filaments at Z-discs**.

D is the same kind of rule (something on the Z-disc side has passed a myosin end) but sees almost
none of these cases:
- it tests barbed tips, not patches;
- its radius, 80 nm, equals the resting actin–myosin centre-line spacing, so bound actins sit on
  its boundary;
- it allows 50 nm of overshoot.

Patches at about 117 nm are outside its reach. That is why only about 2% of linked barbed tips
count as buried while patches overshoot by up to 750 nm. Both numbers are correct.

**Confusion 3: an "effective lifetime" was quoted as a lifetime.** The quantity 1/⟨1/lifetime⟩
over link-frames is only the **random-draw** hazard, and it is frame-weighted (length-biased).
The real mature life is about 120 ms (§2). This was presented as 0.64 vs 0.22 s across A-M
angle in two places, and both need correcting:
- the load-vs-angle artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/QiQU9y2QttVVfU2Ya72uSe), which labels it
  "effective lifetime";
- the long PI-paragraph draft.

## 2. Findings (production control `r27ctrl`, 27 heads/half, seed 20260805, unless noted)

**ACTN2 breakage, 1.0–2.4 s** (93,499 detachments, `actn2_breakage.py`):

| reason | new links (status 1) | mature links (status 2) | all |
|---|---|---|---|
| random draw from the lifetime | 63.6% | 52.6% | 58.5% |
| overstretch (connector > 120 nm) | 32.2% | 46.9% | 39.0% |
| barbed-tip burial (D) | 4.1% | 0.5% | 2.5% |

Overstretch is a jump, not a creep. In the last saved frame (< 5 ms before rupture), 94% of new
links and 65% of mature links were still under 100 nm.

**Overstretch is driven by the hard projection** (`stretch_an.py`, `stretch_an2.py`). In the
frame of an overstretch, one actin moves 88 nm (new links) or 46 nm (mature) while its partner
moves 16–18 nm; random actins move 10 nm. That "mover" had projection events in 74% (new) / 57%
(mature) of cases, against 12% for random actins. Projected movers moved 129 / 96 nm, unprojected
ones 19 / 24 nm. The projection moves the two actins of the offending link; their roughly ten
*other* links are not moved, so they stretch.

**Measured life of mature links** (`life_decomp.py`, events only, link IDs verified unique). Mean
is Kaplan–Meier, restricted to 1 s; angle is A-M at maturation:

| A-M angle at maturation | 0–10° | 10–20° | 20–40° | 40–60° | 60–90° | no strong head |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean mature life | 168 ms | 146 ms | 133 ms | 119 ms | 115 ms | 109 ms |
| share of deaths by overstretch | 48% | 47% | 45% | 49% | 47% | 49% |

Overstretch is an angle-blind hazard of about 4/s, as large as the random-draw hazard. It caps
any catch-bond selection.

**Catch bond.** The lifetime is mature life = 0.15 s + 0.4 s/pN × load, where the load is the
productive opposed load (`sarcomere_actn2.cpp` ~1460–1500; `catch_bond_kinetics.h:14`). The load
is read **instantaneously**, and each step a link breaks with probability dt/lifetime
(`catch_bond_kinetics.h:35`).
- Mean load falls 6.0 → 0.7 pN with A-M angle, and 1.8 → 0.6 pN with A-A.
- Mature links are unloaded 56% of the time. Half of that is an actin with no strong head; the
  rest is heads on the non-productive half (18% of unloaded frames) or a backward net pull (32%).
- Any lifetime(load) law has a ceiling: as the catch strengthens, mature life → 0.15 s ÷
  (unloaded fraction). That gives **2.0× (0–10° vs 20–40°)** against 1.7× today
  (`load_form_est.py`).
- `lifetime_coeff=2` already reaches 1.9×. Lowering the base lifetime gives the same contrast but
  collapses all lifetimes.

**Status 2 never returns to status 1.** There are 80,840 events 1→2, 76,542 events 2→0, and none
2→1. This is set by `aa_status2_locked_until_detach=true`. The dormant unlock path
(`aa_status2_unlock_rate`) requires that lock to be false (`main.cpp:889`). All four parameters
are resume-guarded.

**What this says about the goal.** Removing the projection does remove most of the overstretch.
But as configured, the soft repulsion (A) and the deletion rules (D, E) do not stop thick
filaments overshooting Z-discs:
- **A is not deliverable.** On 10-20 its 20 pN maximum was found to exceed the actin force cap
  (`max_actin_force` 7.13 pN), which binds on 87–90% of actins every step. Its delivered mean was
  0.37 pN. Whether the myosin side is also capped has not been checked.
- **D doesn't reach the patches.** It looks at barbed tips within 80 nm, while the patches sit at
  about 117 nm.

So making "soft repulsion + deletion rule" work needs two decisions:
1. how to make the repulsion deliverable inside the force budget. The 10-20 suggestion was a
   narrower onset with higher stiffness, rather than a larger maximum;
2. what the deletion rule keys on. Widening D toward 130 nm, or keying on the patch, would delete
   Z-disc links whenever a myosin overshoots.

Both are code/model changes, so **propose a plan first**.

- **Projection off.** Overstretch falls 57% (mature) and 71% (new links), and mature links live
  17% longer.
  - The cost: thick filaments overshoot Z-discs (median 82 nm, up to 746 nm), and burial deaths
    rise.
  - Z-band order and myosin order are a little lower. That is within plausible single-run scatter
    and needs replicates.
  - The projection phase itself was 0.048–0.055 s per step in the projection-on arms.
  - Total wall time can't be compared: the control was preempted and restarted, and its segment
    on the same CPU model spent heavily on I/O.
- **`lifetime_coeff=2` is a null**, as expected while overstretch dominates. Mature life goes
  118 → 123 ms and overstretch is unchanged. Mature life across A-M angle is flat: 125 ms at
  0–10° (n = 109) against 123 ms at 60–90°.
- **Implication for the goal.** Dropping the projection fixes most of the crosslink overstretch.
  What it leaves undone is stopping thick filaments at Z-discs. That is the job the
  soft-repulsion + deletion-rule replacement has to do (§3).
