## Executive summary

**The headline is the force cap, and it was found last.** `max_actin_force` is
**7.127 pN** and it binds on **87–90% of actins at every step**
(697,897,169 cap events over 500,000 steps = 0.872 per actin per step). For
~88% of actins the force actually applied is not the force the model computed;
it is a renormalised 7.13 pN. The cap is not independently tunable — it is
`Δx_max/(μ·dt)` = 0.005/(64 × 1e-5) ≈ 7.8 pN, matching the recorded value.

This qualifies most force-based reasoning below. My notes already flag
`am_bidirectional_crossbridge` as unusable because it "clamps 7–53% of steps";
production is running at 87–90%, far past that threshold.

**`am_motor_saturation_heads=6` works, and 27 heads beats 54.** At matched
frame (514/515, seed 0805) against base, with **only `msat` differing**:

| | base | 27h msat6 |
|---|---|---|
| ACTN2 status-2 links | 2121 | **4101** (1.93×) |
| M order | 0.258 | **0.669** (2.6×) |
| Z order | 0.210 | **0.824** (3.9×) |
| strong heads / actin | 2.588 | 2.857 |

Strong heads are nearly identical, so the gain is the force normalisation, not
engagement. The 27-head arm at **half** the simulated time exceeds the completed
54-head arm on every structural measure (M 0.669 vs 0.530, Z 0.824 vs 0.667,
status-2 links 4101 vs 2875, unlinked actins 352 vs 718).

**The banding lands at the right period.** Splitting spatial harmonics
(Lx = 6 µm, so n=3 is a 2 µm sarcomere period), 54h msat6 peaks at **n=3 with
amplitude 0.632** for barbed tips and **0.509** for myosin centres — both
species agreeing, at ~2× base's best. Base cannot decide: seed 0805 peaks at
n=4 (1.5 µm, wrong period), seed 0815's tips say n=3 but its myosins say n=4.
Caveat: 54h also carries a substantial n=4 component (0.458), which is *not* a
harmonic of 3, so the register is 2 µm with a 1.5 µm admixture.

**The bare-tip endstop is redundant and mis-aimed.** See its own section.

## Corrections

1. **"Crosslinks die by non-replacement" is minimal-rig only.** In A1600/M320
   Lx=6 production the ACTN2 population builds monotonically and never dies:
   status-2/actin 0.46 → 3.02, peaking at t=4.55 s, retaining 98% of peak,
   max post-peak drawdown 3%. `am_overlap_available` stays flat at 1.07–1.16 µm
   — there is no 1.44 → 0.33 µm gate collapse. I framed a production A/B as a
   retention rescue on the strength of that note. Memory corrected in place.

2. **A `diff -rq` against a missing path reports success.** I concluded "the
   source trees are identical" from a diff pointed at
   `prod_steric080_20260930/cpp`, which does not exist (the snapshot is at
   `source_snapshot/cpp`). The real diff is 2 altered lines in `src/` and 0
   removed in `include/`. Stat both roots before trusting any diff.

3. **"Owners requeue clobbers artifacts" does not apply to this script.**
   `submit_hdprod.sbatch` guards on `SLURM_RESTART_COUNT` and passes
   `--resume=true`. Measured: task `48428280_2` was preempted **4×** and resumed
   from 515, 515, 552, 555 committed frames, never from step 0. Memory scoped.

4. **"Uncrosslinked actins are essentially static" was an engagement confound.**
   The 0-crosslink bin was dominated by actins carrying almost no heads (motor
   force 1.07 vs 3.6 pN). Controlling for strong-head count reverses it: at
   matched engagement more crosslinks mean **monotonically slower** inward
   sliding — 54h at strong=1 runs −0.412 (AA=0) → −0.114 µm/s (AA 8–12), a
   3.6× brake. Correlations at fixed engagement: 27h +0.021, 54h +0.019.

5. **Column 8 of `local_link_events_v3` is not the link lifetime.** My means
   (169 / 322 / 1486 ms) disagree with Little's law by 2–30× and the
   distribution piles up at exactly 0.1 and 0.15 — a quantised artifact.
   **Use Little's law instead**: live links ÷ formation rate gives 84.1 (base),
   62.4 (27h), 49.2 ms (54h), and those reproduce the observed counts exactly.

6. **The per-pair-vs-per-actin decomposition was inverted.** I first reported
   the rig-vs-production gap as 1.37× attachment × 2.28× graduation by dividing
   a **myosin-side** attached count by an **actin-side** strong count. On a
   consistent single-partner actin-side basis it is **2.66× attachment × 1.39×
   graduation** — attachment dominant, the opposite of what I said.

7. **The dose–response is real; my "flat" claim used the wrong N.** Regressing
   per-*pair* angle on per-*actin* strong count averages each actin's well- and
   poorly-aligned pairs together and washes it out. With a true per-pair N it is
   **−5.76°/head** (base), −5.91 (27h), −3.95 (54h), reproducing the original
   48° → 12.35° slope. The matched-N cross-arm comparison is nonetheless
   invalid: N=4 is a 1.9% event in base and a 19.9% event in 54h, so those bins
   are different percentiles of their populations.

8. **The endstop precision figure was overstated ~10×.** My classifier used the
   bare capsule surface; the force engages at `signed_clearance < onset`, i.e.
   50 nm **outside** it. Counting the buffer, precision on buried crosslinks is
   **0.0598** (base) and **0.2483** (54h), not 0.013/0.020.

9. **Three proposed fixes were wrong before being built.** (a) load-dependent
   `k_ps` — the force–velocity relation already exists on the stroke advance
   (`working_stroke_velocity`, V_max 5 µm/s, stall 4.5 pN); the inert
   `load_slowed_rate(…, 0.0, …)` is on the weak→strong *recruitment* rate, a
   different transition. (b) `am_overlap_target_ratio` — the throttle is
   non-local and goal-directed (a head cannot know its pair's total overlap);
   the code calls it "the prescribed model". (c) `k_mm > 0` — anchoring thick
   filaments during assembly assumes the structure the run is meant to produce.

## The bare-tip endstop

What it computes, from `interaction.cpp:1233`. Barbed tip **b** = `actin.left_end`,
myosin centre **c**, unit axis **û**, `r = b − c` (min-image):

```
s      = r · û                                  axial coordinate
d⊥     = ‖r − s û‖                              transverse distance
                                                radial gate: d⊥ < R_cap
e      = √(R_cap² − d⊥²)                        hemispherical end cap
S      = L_m/2 + e                              capsule axial boundary
Δ      = |s| − S                                signed clearance
χ      = onset − Δ                              compression (only if Δ < onset)
F      = F_max · tanh(k χ / F_max)
F_actin = F · sign(s) · û                       AXIAL, outward
```

with `R_cap = min(aa_occlusion_capture_radius, capture_centerline) = 0.080`,
`onset = 0.050`, `k = 1000 pN/µm`, `F_max = 20 pN`, `L_m = 1.5 µm`.

**Three things are wrong with it as sterics.** The push is **axial**, where real
excluded volume acts along the line of closest approach. `R_cap = 0.080` is
**1.6× the myosin radius** (0.050) and 23% wider than hard contact (0.065). And
at the measured operating offset `d⊥ ≈ 0.0765`, `e ≈ 0.024`, so the force
engages at `|s| < 0.824 µm` — **anywhere inside the filament's own span plus
74 nm beyond it** (unit test T4 confirms 0.8234 µm).

**It fires on bare tips, hardest.** Contrary to the 2026-09-28 design note
("needs a live ACTN2 link"), only `aa_zdisc_tip_contact_count` is link-gated;
the force is not:

| | frac with force > 0 | mean force | tip_contact_count |
|---|---|---|---|
| base, 0 links | **0.791** | **7.32 pN** | 0.000 |
| base, ≥1 link | 0.309 | 2.37 pN | 0.197 |
| 54h, 0 links | 0.551 | 7.80 pN | 0.000 |
| 54h, ≥1 link | 0.249 | 2.67 pN | 0.205 |

**It does not target buried crosslinks.** The ACTN2 attachment point sits up to
`aa_barbed_zone` (0.2 µm) inward of the tip, so the two conditions differ. Over
524,027 and 207,644 link-arms, with the onset buffer counted:

| | base | 54h |
|---|---|---|
| precision (forced arms whose crosslink is in the engagement region) | 0.060 | 0.248 |
| crosslink clearance past the capsule, median | **+0.057 µm** | **+0.131 µm** |
| barbed tip \|s\| median | 0.606 (89.5% inside) | 0.865 (22.2% inside) |

The crosslink typically sits **outside** the engagement region while the tip is
inside — the tip is reliably ~0.2 µm deeper than the thing the force exists to
protect.

**And it is redundant.** Two other live mechanisms key on the actual attachment
point via `compute_actn2_patch_geometry(actin, i, j, s_i, s_j, box)`:

| | mechanism | activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | soft patch force (`sarcomere_actn2.cpp:2282`) | 11.2% of actin-frames, 0.37 pN mean |
| 2 | **hard position projection** (`langevin.cpp:361`) | **17.45 M projections / 5 s ≈ 35 per step**, 1.79 nm each |
| 3 | bare-tip endstop | 48.0% of actin-frames, **4.10 pN mean** |

Mechanisms 1 and 3 overlap on only 6.2% of actin-frames. **The endstop is the
dominant force of the three by 11×, and it is the one aimed at the wrong point.**

Note the in-code comment above the projection predicate claims it "fires ~2
times per step out of ~53,000 evaluations". Measured at production density it is
**~35 projections per step, 17× the documented rate** — the comment appears to
date from the minimal rig, and it is the justification for the cheap-predicate
short-circuit.

**Why the soft force cannot substitute.** `aa_zdisc_tip_force_max` is 20 pN
against a 7.127 pN total-force cap — **2.8× undeliverable**, sharing that budget
with motors and sterics. Delivered mean: 0.37 pN. The projection exists because
the force is clipped.

## Mechanism chain (provisional — see the force-cap caveat)

Measured end to end for `msat`, at 27 heads:

1. per-pair axial force flattened: `d|F|/d(bound heads)` **+0.536 → +0.105 pN**
   at P=1 (5.1×), per-actin mean force 0.97 → 0.58 pN
2. directed sliding abolished: net **+0.1403 ± 0.0200 (7.0σ) → +0.0007 ± 0.0139
   (0.1σ)** µm/s
3. motion becomes purely diffusive: |displacement| grows **5.64×** over a 32×
   lag (√32 = 5.66); base grows 7.99×, i.e. superdiffusive
4. barbed-end zone stays exposed, and exposure is what sets crosslinking:

| free barbed zone | <0.02 µm | 0.06–0.12 µm |
|---|---|---|
| 27h status-2 / actin | 1.44 | **7.74** (5.4×) |
| 54h status-2 / actin | 1.07 | **6.26** (5.8×) |

**Two caveats on the sliding result.** The 30% drop in |v_slide| is **not**
pair-specific — shuffling partners gives the same ratio (0.68 vs 0.70), so it is
a global slowdown. And base's net sliding is only ~40% pair-specific (shuffled
null +0.0833, 4.0σ, against a true +0.1403).

### Why 54 heads does not help

Head layout is a pure **density** doubling: the head-bearing span is fixed at
`0.45 × 1.5 = 0.675 µm` per half, so crowns go 9 → 18 and spacing 75 → 37.5 nm.
`am_sites_per_actin = 182` (~5.5 nm) is never limiting; the binding constraint is
`am_max_heads_per_actin = 24`, at which **8.2% of 54h actin-frames sit pinned**
(39.7% within 75% of it) versus 0.10% at 27 heads.

Lifecycle decomposition via Little's law closes to 0.5%:

| 54h vs 27h | ratio |
|---|---|
| formation rate (87,583 → 69,307 /s) | 0.791× |
| mean lifetime (62.4 → 49.2 ms) | 0.788× |
| product | **0.623×** |
| observed link ratio | **0.623×** |

Kitagawa split of the link deficit against AM engagement: **0.794× composition ×
0.776× rate = 0.616×** vs 0.609× observed. Engagement and strong-head count are
nearly collinear, so "composition" is largely head count re-expressed; the
contribution of that analysis is naming the shared resource —
`engaged/available` barbed overlap, 0.724 → 0.873, with **61% of 54h actins above
90% occupancy** versus 33%.

Downstream geometry: burial requires the actin centre within
`L_m/2 − L_a/2 = 0.25 µm` of the myosin centre. 54h has **19.6%** of
near-parallel pairs inside that window versus 6.5%; **zero** pairs with offset
≥ 0.5 µm are buried, in either arm. 54h's pointed end overshoots the M-line by
99 nm (27h: +0.005 µm, i.e. parked on it), 59.2% get past the 75 nm bare zone
(27h: 29.6%), and its barbed tip retracts 0.976 → 0.883 µm — across the 0.880 µm
capture envelope, halving the free barbed zone (0.093 → 0.050 µm) and leaving
**58.8% of pairs with none** versus 29.9%.

### Why base shows no crosslink brake

Its crosslinks are torn, not held: **31.05%** of base removals are at or past the
0.120 µm rupture length (p95 length 0.2045 µm = 2.6× rest), with tension at
removal 21.5 pN (p95 **89.0**). 27h: 11.68% and 8.5 pN. A link being stretched
to failure cannot anchor anything — which is the real justification for the
force cap, unphysical as the mechanism is.

### Rig vs production

At identical `hph` and identical kinetics (activation is 1.0000 in both;
`k_on`/`k_strong_off`/`k_weak_off` and the `q_strong` parameters all match), the
minimal rig carries **3.51×** the strong heads per pair: 2.66× more attached ×
1.39× higher strong fraction, with 3.92 vs 6.43 actins per myosin half.

Strong fraction is set by `k_ps/(k_ps + k_strong_off)` with
`k_ps = am_k_powerstroke × q` = 200 × 0.772 → predicted ceiling **0.491**. The rig
measures 0.498 (98% of ceiling); production 0.377 (77%).

## Built this session (`zdisclink_20261020`, 42/42 tests pass)

| flag | default | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `aa_zdisc_tip_contact_requires_link` | `false` | endstop applies only to actins carrying a live ACTN2 link |
| `aa_zdisc_tip_hard_projection` | `true` | `false` removes the position projection; penetration then kills the link via `actn2_myosin_collision` |

The second flag was necessary because `resolve_actn2_tip_hard_contacts`
early-returns on `!model.aa_zdisc_tip_contact` — the same flag that gates the
soft force — so the projection could not previously be removed without also
removing the force.

**Gate implementation.** `aa_links_by_actin[i]` non-empty ⇔ actin *i* carries a
live link. Binding (`:561`) runs **before** the link rebuild (`:598`), so this
reads the previous step's committed state — deliberate, to avoid a mid-update
ordering dependence. Bridge links push both partners into the bucket, and ACTN2
capture is already confined to the barbed zone, so "has a link" is "has a link
on the barbed end" — the same predicate titin uses
(`formation_requires_live_actn2_on_barbed_end_any_status`). Gated at
*application*, so clearance/penetration diagnostics stay populated.

**Verification.**
- flag off ⇒ **288/288 datasets bit-identical** to the production binary, both
  with the flag absent and explicitly `false`; flag on ⇒ 228/288 differ
- behaviourally: force on unlinked actins **27.4% → 0.16%** (171×), linked
  actins still served
- the one-step lag is fully accounted: at `save_every=1`, of 355 force events,
  **210 have a link now, 145 had one the previous step, 0 unexplained**
- new unit test `zdisc_tip_endstop_test.cpp` (7 checks) pins the force law at
  1600 configurations, the radial gate, the capsule boundary, the onset offset,
  axial/equal-and-opposite direction, monotonicity and `F_max` saturation

`aa_zdisc_tip_hard_projection` verification, job `48696715`, **COMPLETED**:

```
new_default  datasets=277 differing=0    IDENTICAL
noproj       datasets=277 differing=217  DIFFERS
old     flag=ABSENT  removals=624  projections=1258
        reasons: {1: 48.56%, 2: 0.16%, 3: 50.64%, 4: 0.64%}
noproj  flag=0       removals=509  projections=0
        reasons: {1: 49.31%, 2: 0.59%, 3: 50.10%, 4: 0.00%}
```

Defaults are a verified drop-in and the flag does disable the projection
(1258 → 0). **But reason 4 went 0.64% → 0.00%, the opposite of the prediction.**

**So "if the crosslink enters the myosin, it's dead" is NOT implemented for the
axial direction.** `compute_actn2_myosin_contact` is called only under
`may_contact_body` — `actn2_patch_may_contact_myosin_**body**` — so reason 4 is a
**radial** hard-core penetration test (`hard_core_overlap` from
`contact.penetration > 0.0`). Radial overlap is separately prevented by
`_volume_exclusion`, and the earlier note already recorded reason 4 as
"geometrically unreachable at this radial offset" (85 nm radial vs a 65 nm hard
core). Removing the **axial** projection creates axial penetrations, which that
test does not see — so the crosslink simply sits inside the thick filament with
nothing happening.

Implementing the rule therefore **does need new code**: an axial
penetration → death path, keyed on the patch's `axial_gap` (the same quantity
`zdisc_myosin_tip_gap` already computes for the projection) rather than on
radial `penetration`. That is the natural companion to
`aa_zdisc_tip_hard_projection=false` and should land with it.

**Detachment reason distribution in production** (base, column 30, 244,662
removals): 30.02% geometric_contour, 56.66% stochastic, 12.06%
am_overlap_occlusion, **1.26% actn2_myosin_collision**. Do not compare the
200-step verification mix above to this — at 200 steps the stochastic channel
has barely opened (0.16% vs 56.66%) and the distribution is all early
transient.

## Open questions, in the order I would take them

1. **The force cap.** 87–90% clamping makes every force-based conclusion above
   provisional. Measure the delivered-vs-requested force per term before
   anything else. The cap is `Δx_max/(μ·dt)`, so it cannot be raised without
   scaling the displacement cap or cutting `dt`.
2. **Write the axial death path**, then run `aa_zdisc_tip_hard_projection=false`.
   As built, removing the projection leaves penetrating crosslinks alive and
   buried (reason 4 is radial-only and goes to 0.00%, measured). The missing
   piece is a deletion keyed on the patch's `axial_gap`, which
   `zdisc_myosin_tip_gap` already computes. Expect a severe crosslink loss once
   it exists — that is the honest consequence of the rule, not a failure.
3. **Can the soft force work inside the existing budget?** The projection
   corrects only 1.79 nm per event, so the required impulse may be small. Try a
   narrower `aa_zdisc_tip_onset` with higher `aa_zdisc_tip_stiffness` rather
   than a larger `F_max`, which is already undeliverable.
4. **Is the 5 s campaign converged?** The barbed-tip passage has a 16–31 s
   timescale (drift 0.061–0.109 µm/s over a 1.79–1.93 µm exit distance), so that
   degree of freedom cannot be at steady state. "27 heads is better" may be
   "27 heads is better *at 5 s*".
5. **The remaining 22% of the rig-vs-production graduation gap** — attachment
   accounts for 2.66× of 3.51×, and nothing measured explains the rest.
6. **`am_overlap_target_ratio` and `am_bidirectional_crossbridge` are both 0 /
   false in every production run**, as recorded previously. Neither is
   recommended here (Correction 9a/9b) but the fact should stay visible.
