## Executive summary

**One parameter turns the minimal wrap rig into a stable minimal sarcomere.**
`am_binding_azimuth_half_width` 30° → 180° (`azfree_5s_20260915`, job
`43643271`) differs from the standard rig in *exactly one* of 278 recorded
attributes, and it takes the fraction of frames with both ACTN2 actin
pairs mature from **20.0% to 99.9%**, P(≥2 bound heads) from **0.472 to 0.984**, and the
time at which the structure is lost from **1087 ms to never**. Three seeds, no
exceptions. This is the single most useful result of the session and it was
sitting in a diagnostic arm that the 09-15 framing had written off.

**The deterministic force that pushes actin off myosin is a reference-frame
conflict between two radial springs, and it is model-wide.** `am_head_transverse_k`
measures `rho` transverse to the **actin** axis from a discrete surface anchor
against a 30 nm rest; `am_tether_k` measures **centreline** closest approach
against an 80 nm rest. Because the head's rest length is not the tether's rest
length minus anything geometric, they cannot both be satisfied: the head sits
**+13.9 nm stretched** in 95.8% of bound heads (pulling in, −2.52 pN) while the
tether sits **−4.3 nm compressed** in 98.8% of frames (pushing out, +2.77 pN),
for a **net +0.15 pN outward in 6/6 runs, including at `thermal_noise_scale = 0`**.
Production shows the same −19.7 nm consistency error over 36,645 instances.
The compressive branch is inherited from a legacy harmonic;
`ACCELERATED_DYNAMICS_FITTING.md:285-287` already flagged the intent ("a
'flat-bottom containment' spring should be idle most of the time") and the 2 nm
dead zone is roughly 2× too narrow.

**The motor was invisible in every force table produced on 09-15, and that was
an instrumentation defect.** The `am_head` bucket summed the radial retention
spring and the axial power stroke, and only the radial projection was ever
reported. Split (job `43724682`): the motor pulls at **2.82 pN** (slack 2 nm)
to **4.65 pN** (slack 15 nm) per actin and is **99.9% axial**. The split is
exact — `radial + axial` reproduces the old bucket with a closure error of
4×10⁻¹⁶.

**The wrap rig's dynamics are free rod diffusion to within 1%,** which is why so
little in it is informative. Frame-to-frame displacement rate averages
13.72 µm/s against a no-forces-at-all projection of 15.05 µm/s. Worse, the rig
**starts perfectly aligned (0.0°)**, so nothing in it has ever tested
spontaneous alignment — only retention.

## Key measurements

### The azimuth clamp is the stability knob

`az_base` vs the standard rig, 3 seeds, whole 5 s. Single-attribute diff
confirmed against all 278 recorded parameters.

| MEASURED | azimuth ±30° | azimuth free (180°) |
|---|---:|---:|
| crosslinked actin **pairs**, mature (of 2) | 0.42 | **2.00** |
| mature ACTN2 **links** (of 6; 3 per pair) | 1.07 | **5.82** |
| frames with both pairs mature | 20.0% | **99.9%** |
| frames with zero mature links | 78.4% | **0.1%** |
| structure lost at | 1087 ms | **never** |
| P(≥1 bound head) | 0.524 | **0.999** |
| P(≥2 bound heads) | 0.472 | **0.984** |
| P(≥3 bound heads) | 0.409 | **0.854** |
| angle to nearest myosin at 5 s | 39.1° | **9.2°** |
| **torque-cap saturation** | 0.218 | **0.999** ← invalidates rates |

**Counter units matter here.** Two ACTN2 counters exist and they do not count
the same object. `state/aa_status_current` over `aa_pairs_current_count` counts
crosslinked actin **pairs** (max 2 in this rig); `catch_bond/local_link_identity`
column 4 counts individual **links** under the live multilink system
(`aa_local_multilink=true`, `aa_prebundle_links_per_pair=3`, so max 6). They
agree exactly on the alive fraction (0.999 and 0.216 by either counter) and
differ by ~2.3-2.9x in magnitude. At production scale the same file gives 1196
by the pair counter and 2741 by the link counter. State which one you used.

The ±30° clamp is not modelled physics: `select_am_binding_azimuth`
(`interaction.cpp:695`, clamp at `:729`) computes the aimed azimuth and then
truncates it.
The clamp saturates in **81.4%** of bound heads in the rig and **65.1%** in
production. But 180° is the opposite extreme — a head may reach right around the
filament — so where on the ladder stability appears is an open question, not a
settled choice.

### The alignment couple is not the scarce ingredient

Measured per arm from `am_head_target_axial_current` (the attachment coordinate
along the actin, ±0.5 µm), with ⟨κ⟩ = P₂·k_ρ·⟨dz²⟩/2 expressed in kT:

| arm | P₂ | dz given ≥2 (nm) | ⟨κ⟩ (kT) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `reach_a4_h27c100` | 0.784 | 548.0 | 3228 |
| `sl_s08` | 0.539 | 358.1 | 904 |
| `nn_base` (standard) | 0.472 | 350.7 | 801 |
| `az_base` | 0.984 | 148.0 | 483 |
| `md_ctl` (effectively dead) | 0.010 | 168.2 | 4.1 |

Every arm is **2–3 orders of magnitude above the ~1 threshold**, including the
dead ones. Connector multiplicity and span are therefore *not* what limits
alignment in this rig; the ability of the connectors to aim is. Note also that
`az_base` has the **lowest** dz of the listed arms and the **best** alignment —
further evidence that κ has stopped discriminating in this regime.

`am_alignment_k = 0` in **49 of 50 arms** (only the original `wrap_base` carried
120), so the no-alignment-potential condition is already satisfied throughout.

### Displacement rate vs time — all 150 wrap-rig runs

Definition: ‖min-image(r(t+Δt) − r(t))‖ / Δt per body, averaged over bodies.
Δt read per file from `(current_step[f+1] − current_step[f])·dt` = exactly
5.000 ms in all 150 runs. Minimum imaging is mandatory or a boundary crossing
reads as a box-sized jump.

PROJECTED free-diffusion ceiling, from `rod_mobility::finite_cylinder` with each
run's own parameters (L = 1 µm, d = 10 nm hydrodynamic — *not* 2×radius, p = 100,
η = 1 mPa·s, T = 310 K, crowding 10): D∥ = 0.3003, D⊥ = 0.1855 µm²/s, giving
**15.05 µm/s** for actin and **7.92 µm/s** for myosin over a 5 ms window.

| MEASURED | 0–500 ms | 4500–5000 ms | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| rig baseline (noise 1.0) | 8.13 | 14.89 | 1.83 |
| slack 15 nm | 5.67 | 11.85 | 2.09 |
| noise 0.3 | 6.49 | 6.02 | 0.93 |
| noise 0 (deterministic) | 6.41 | 3.36 | 0.52 |
| actin mobility frozen | 2.70 | 2.57 | 0.95 |
| 27 heads / 100 nm cutoff | 28.65 | 38.60 | 1.35 |

Three things follow. **(1)** Split at ACTN2 extinction across 15 seed-runs,
actin goes from **55–62% to 96–100%** of free diffusion and myosin from **77% to
98%** — both species are held while the network lives and become free rods when
it dies. **(2)** The zero-noise residual is pure deterministic drift: 4.2 µm/s
overall, of which the transverse part implies ~0.10 pN through
μ⊥ = 43.34 µm/(s·pN) — independent corroboration of the +0.15 pN net outward
force measured from the force buckets. **(3)** The 27-head/100 nm arms run at
**2× the free-diffusion ceiling**, i.e. force-driven; treat that whole campaign
as an inflated-reach artifact.

Because this is a rate over a finite window and diffusion scales as Δt^(−1/2),
these numbers belong to Δt = 5 ms. All 150 runs share it, so comparisons are
valid; absolute values are not portable to another save interval.

### Why ACTN2 lets go — it does not break

From `catch_bond/local_link_events_v3`, 3 seeds per arm. **82–91% of
detachments are `stochastic`.** At the moment of release the links are
antiparallel to `direction_dot = −0.9998`, **82–86 nm long against a 120 nm
rupture limit**, carrying 1.5–1.8 pN. Nothing fails mechanically. The timer
fires at the bare **0.15 s floor** because `effective_load` reads **median
exactly 0.0000** despite that tension — the status-2 retention defect. With
detachments outrunning associations (82 vs 64 in s02; 119 vs 101 in s15) the
population decays to extinction and cannot re-form past the 118.6 nm gate.

Post-collapse force budget (s15, 3 seeds, pN per actin): `actn2_spring`
18.54 → **0.000**, `aa_lateral` 3.79 → **0.000**, `am_head` 5.17 → 3.87,
`titin` 0.61 → **3.67 (6.0×)**, `am_tether` 0.55 → 0.77, sterics 0.31 → 0.31.
Sum 28.99 → 8.63 pN. **77% of the budget disappears as the two ACTN2 channels go
to exactly zero**, and titin rises to become co-equal with the heads.

### Cap saturation is pervasive and under-reported

`cumulative_torque_cap_events` at the final frame divided by `n_steps`, meaned
over actins: **39 of 50 arms exceed 0.2**, and seven arms sit at **0.98–0.999**
(`az_base`/`az_n0` 0.999, `md_ps` 0.997, `wrap_base` 0.994, `md_ctl`/`s2_*`/
`reach_a1`/`wrap_sticky` 0.982). Since F_max = Δx_max/(µ·dt), cap counts are
circular — the caps must be scaled with dt rather than read as physics. Any
rotation *rate* or alignment *timescale* from a saturated arm is an artifact of
`max_actin_rotation = 0.005`.

### The rig cannot measure alignment by nematic order

Monte-Carlo floor for the eigenvalue nematic S of **4** random rods (20,000
draws): mean **0.432**, p95 **0.705**. Measured S sits inside that floor in
**45 of 50 arms**. The only arms above it are `az_base`/`az_n0` at S ≈ 0.996 —
and those are the 99.9%-cap-saturated ones. With 4 filaments, S is not an
instrument. Use the angle to the nearest myosin, and report it as a trajectory
from a known initial angle.

## Corrections to earlier conclusions

- **`k_mm` (myomesin) is NOT dead code any more.** The 09-15 and earlier notes
  say the M-band spring computes forces and discards them. Verified false against
  `slack_5s_20260916/source_snapshot`: a sparse finite-valency bridge model
  replaced the old spring. `_apply_myomesin_bridge` (`sarcomere.cpp:7418`)
  accumulates into `local_myosin_forces[...]` = `myosin_forces_temp[thread]`,
  the live accumulator; `_volume_exclusion` drives it over `myomesin_bridges`;
  `_update_myomesin_bridges` (`:7276`, called from `:803`) manages attachment. The radial term now
  has a real rest length (`myomesin_radial_rest`), so the old "it would collapse
  every myosin onto one plane" objection is also void. **The mechanism is
  untested, not broken** — every production sbatch passes `--k_mm=0`, so it has
  never been exercised. "M-band coupling doesn't help" is an unestablished claim.
- **The 09-15 crowding framing is refuted.** "The actins are simply diffusing
  too fast, so escape is an artifact to remove" does not survive: escape still
  happens at **`thermal_noise_scale = 0`**, freezing actin mobility *hurts* duty
  (22.9% → 15.3%), and freezing both species is worst (8.34%). Mobility gates
  recapture as much as it gates escape. Treat the crowding bracket as a null,
  not as an unfinished lead.
- **`az_base` demonstrates alignment *retention*, not spontaneous alignment.**
  The rig initialises at **0.0°** (`minimal_catch_balanced` with
  `actin_direction_noise = 0`, which that builder ignores anyway). The 9.2°
  endpoint is "never left", not "rotated in". The 09-15 executive summary's
  "answered **yes**" stands only for the *confined tilted* rig (job `43436344`),
  which starts at 60°/85°. Nothing in the wrap-rig family tests it.
- **`actn2_catchbond_5s_20260912` is not a 5 s campaign.** All three tasks were
  killed by a signal at the same second on 2026-09-12, at 0.7–3% of 10⁶ steps.
  The files still say `5000ms` but hold 31, 31 and 150 frames. Any result quoted
  from that directory is void. The only complete 5 s production runs are
  `span_noalign_5s_20260910`.
- **The `s2retention` arms are replicates of their own control.** `s2_ctl`,
  `s2_sm10` and `s2_sm60` differ only at 1×10⁻¹⁰–1×10⁻⁵ — the
  `--aa_status2_smoothed_retention` flag does not exist in the binary those runs
  used. They are three copies of one configuration, not a sweep. Same for
  `md_ctl` ≡ `wrap_sticky` ≡ `reach_a1_h9c50` (identical summary statistics to 3
  decimals), and `cw_c400par` ≡ `fz_apar`.
- **A single time-averaged rate can hide a step.** The myosin run-mean is 7.86
  µm/s = 99% of free diffusion, which reads as "myosin is never constrained".
  Splitting at ACTN2 extinction shows 77% → 98%. Always split on the structural
  event before averaging over a run.
- **The rig's Z-disc is 2 pairs / 6 links, not "two bonds".** An earlier figure
  annotation in this session said "this rig has at most 2 mature links in total,
  so the whole Z-disc is two bonds". That was the pair counter mislabelled as
  links; with `aa_prebundle_links_per_pair=3` there are up to 6.
- **Retracted within the session: "slack 8 nm is the sweet spot because its cap
  saturation is below 0.2".** 0.198 ± 0.140 against 0.387 ± 0.085 does not
  support it.

Carried forward unchanged from 09-15 and still true: escape is ~5.8 ms not
34 ms; initial A–M separation is 80.0 nm; capture is gated on excess extension
(~130 nm centreline reach, ~180 nm rupture); per-actin occupancy not an OR;
`am_tether_occupancy_floor` is dead code behind `if (am_bonds[i][j] != 1)`
(`sarcomere.cpp:6600`); outcomes are seed-determined, so add seeds not
replicates.

## Open questions

1. **Where on the azimuth ladder does stability appear?** 30° gives 20% mature
   links, 180° gives 99.9%. 60° and 90° are unmeasured, and the physical
   question — whether a head may reach around the filament at all — is
   unresolved. This is the highest-value cheap experiment available.
2. **Does the multi-connector couple actually align a *tilted* actin inside the
   wrap rig?** Cannot be answered today: `actin_direction_noise` is only read
   under `initial_structure=myosin_x_noise`, and `minimal_catch_variant()` takes
   a variant string with no tilt argument. Needs either a small
   `minimal_catch_tilt_deg` parameter (precedent: `three_membered_tilt_jitter`)
   or a `geometry_h5` import of a pre-rotated pose.
3. **What do the caps do to the answer?** `az_base` at 0.999 torque saturation
   must be re-run with `max_actin_rotation` raised (or dt lowered with caps
   scaled) before any alignment timescale is quotable. This is a prerequisite
   arm, not a result.
4. **What does `k_mm > 0` do?** Never run. Standing candidate for the missing
   second registration plane and for the coarsening defect (domains internally
   S = 0.95–0.99 with directors 42–65° apart). Costs a parameter, not a code
   change. Confirm bridges actually form before trusting a first result.
5. **Does peak-hold-with-decay fix status-2 retention?** The EMA attempt reached
   0.0160 pN of a needed 1.06 because an EMA tracks the mean of a 0.02%-duty
   signal. Must be tested at production scale, where 1196 links exist — the rig
   has 2.
6. **Should the radial-spring structure be repaired rather than tuned?** The
   defensible structure is repulsion-only for the tether (no compressive branch)
   with the head spring carrying retention. This invalidates any calibration
   that leaned on the compressive branch and needs the escape/retention balance
   re-fit. Lower priority than 1–5: +0.15 pN is ~0.5% of a 29 pN budget, though
   it is directional and survives zero noise.
7. Unresolved from 09-15, still unresolved: 14 in-range frames after t = 440 ms
   producing zero binding at near-ideal geometry; one gap episode reporting the
   actin at 738 nm on its last *bound* frame.

## Proposed next rig

Goal: a minimal sarcomere that is (a) structurally stable and (b) tests
spontaneous alignment from multiple AM connectors with `am_alignment_k = 0`.

- **Base**: `minimal_catch_balanced`, `am_alignment_k = 0` (already the default
  in 49/50 arms).
- **Prerequisite arm**: de-saturate the caps — `max_actin_rotation`
  0.005 → 0.02 / 0.05 — and gate on `torque_sat < 0.2`. Nothing downstream is
  interpretable until this passes.
- **Azimuth ladder**: 30 / 60 / 90 / 180°, to locate stability onset rather than
  assume 180.
- **Tilt ladder**: 10 / 30 / 60° initial misalignment. Requires the code change
  in open question 2.
- **Seeds**: ≥6, scored as P(aligns) rather than a mean — these outcomes are
  bistable and n=3 means are meaningless.
- **Readout**: angle(t) from the known initial tilt, with P₁/P₂/P₃, dz, ⟨κ⟩,
  mature-link count and `torque_sat` reported alongside as validity gates.
